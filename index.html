One ledger. Every step of getting paid.

Tally follows the shape of freelance work itself — track, tidy up, send, get paid — instead of bolting time tracking onto software built for someone else's workflow.

Track

Start a timer without opening an app

A single keyboard shortcut starts and stops the clock from anywhere on your machine. Tally notices which app has focus and tags the entry to the right client automatically.

Tidy

Fix a messy week in minutes

Drag entries between projects, split a block across two clients, or merge duplicate timers — your week stays honest without hours of admin.

Send

Invoices write themselves

Approved hours become a line-itemed invoice, branded with your rates and terms, ready to send before your coffee's cold.

Get paid

Clients pay from the invoice itself

Card, bank transfer, or wallet — clients pay right where they read the invoice, and Tally marks the hours settled the moment it clears.
